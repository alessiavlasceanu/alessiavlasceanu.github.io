PhD Researcher · University of Manchester

Alessia M. Vlasceanu

I research the space between human cognition, ethical reasoning, and intelligent systems — asking how human values, moral judgement, and social context can be meaningfully built into the design and evaluation of AI. I combine technical work in programming and data analysis with human-centred research methods to understand how people interact with, and are affected by, AI systems.

Focus areas

What I work on

My PhD sits across three connected threads — trust, ethics, and the people at the centre of it all.

Human-AI Interaction & Trust

Designing and evaluating explainable AI systems that people can actually understand, question, and trust.

Ethics-by-Design

Embedding moral reasoning, human values, and social context into AI systems from the ground up — not bolted on after.

Human-Centred Methods

Mixed-methods research bringing together computational tools and psychological science to study real human behaviour.
